What this device can actually do

Open this page on any device you are considering as a receiver — a phone, a laptop, or a television browser — and press the button. It reports the audio capabilities that matter for synchronisation, and measures the two that are worth measuring rather than trusting: does the audio clock track real time, and does a scheduled start happen when it was asked to.

A tap is required because browsers will not start audio without one, and an audio context that has never run reports nothing useful.
